1.0 — About

About

Taylor Green is a creative technologist and technical director based in Atlanta. He designs show systems, programs media servers and lighting consoles, and helps produce the big moments.

He’s worked with world-class artists in rooms from clubs to stadiums, and travels wherever the show is.

His focus is where lighting and video meet: the systems, servers, and programming that hold a show together. He cares as much about a solid rig as a great-looking show.
