Privacy

This is a static website. It sets no tracking or advertising cookies and runs no analytics.

If you use the contact form, the details you enter (name, email, project details and message) are sent to Web3Forms, which forwards them by email. They are used only to reply to you and are not sold or shared.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request.

To ask about or delete anything you have sent, email hello@taylor.green.
